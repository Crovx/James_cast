## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end haute couture event architecture and experiential luxury. Crafted for discerning clientele seeking bespoke weddings, quinceañeras de gala, high-society baptisms, and premier graduations in Northern Mexico, the interface balances raw architectural discipline with timeless editorial reverence.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **High-Contrast Monochromatic Dramaticism**:
- Stark architectural division: Nocturnal hero environments saturated in rich obsidian contrast directly with gallery-grade crisp white editorial sections.
- Visual storytelling celebrates spatial luxury—monumental chandeliers, bespoke flora, ambient candlelight, and tailored tablescapes—elevated by typography rather than gratuitous embellishment.
- The tone evokes prestige, architectural confidence, and uncompromising curation, establishing James Cast not merely as a coordinator, but as an auteur of unforgettable milestones.

## Colors

The palette embraces strict monochrome rigor warmed by whisper-thin touches of champagne and amber light.

- **Primary (`#0D0D0D` - Obsidian Black):** The anchoring void. Used across immersive night-mode hero stages, immersive galleries, and architectural headers to make photography glow like cinema.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Vintage Champagne Gold):** Deployed sparingly for micro-accents, refined hairline rules, key indicators, and status chips. It mimics candlelight reflecting off cut crystal and polished brass.
- **Tertiary (`#F9F9F8` - Alabaster Paper):** An editorial, high-luminescence off-white that replaces default harsh whites in daylit editorial reading environments, portfolios, and service breakdowns.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1A` - Charcoal Velvet):** Formulated for tertiary cards, subtle elevated surfaces on dark stages, and structural containment borders.

### Color Rules
- Photography provides emotional saturation. UI colors must remain structural and silent.
- Gold is an accent, never a fill for large surface planes. If everything gleams, nothing is luxurious.
- Contrast ratios strictly honor WCAG AAA for editorial typography on both Alabaster and Obsidian surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the imposing, condensed authority of **Anton** with the breath, lucidity, and human geometric balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Anton):** Always treated in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`). Headlines serve as monumental architectural pillars across layouts, recalling fashion monographs and avant-garde architectural publications.
- **Editorial Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set predominantly in Light (`300`) and Regular (`400`) weights to provide a whisper-level contrast against the monolithic Anton headlines. Generous line heights ensure frictionless reading of curatorial project narratives.
- **Overlines & Micro-Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered strictly in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.22em`). Used for categorizing event genres (e.g., `BODAS DE AUTOR`, `QUINCEAÑERAS DE ALTA GAMA`, `GALA PRIVADA`), timestamps, and datum coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 12-column architectural grid with responsive margins and generous vertical cadence.

- **Cadence & Negative Space:** Whitespace is treated as an active luxury material. Spacing between major curated sections ranges from `space-3xl` (96px) to `space-4xl` (128px), imparting an unhurried, galleried exhibition rhythm.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (16px) margins. Display headlines scale down via dedicated mobile tokens. Monolithic imagery converts to full-bleed carousels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, fluid container distribution.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (40px) gutters, capped at `container-max` (1440px) to maintain compositional harmony on ultra-wide viewports.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Staggered, asymmetrical placements—such as a 7-column hero photograph offset against a 4-column typographic description—are preferred over symmetrical card grids.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the minimalist luxury identity, this design system rejects heavy drop shadows and tactile gradients. Depth is established through **architectural stratification, tonal overlays, and hairline delineation**:

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Spatial boundaries are marked by razor-sharp 1px border rules: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` in dark contexts and `rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.08)` in light contexts.
- **Cinematic Vignettes:** On dark canvases, backdrop media layers feature gentle ambient radial overlays (`radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(13, 13, 13, 0) 40%, rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.95) 100%)`) allowing text to rest legibly over high-contrast event photography.
- **Modal & Floating Surfaces:** Overlays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` paired with an ultra-thin champagne hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`), evoking crystal glass and champagne flutes.
- **Shadowless Purity:** Cards and content blocks rest flush on their container surfaces. State changes (hover, focus) rely on scale adjustments and border color transitions rather than vertical shadow elevation.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. 

- All interactive containers, buttons, cards, image viewports, and modals possess zero border-radius (`0px`).
- Rectilinear purity honors editorial print layouts, high-fashion invitations, and modern spatial architecture.
- Structural geometry creates tension with the organic curves present in the photography (chandeliers, floral arrangements, flowing couture gowns).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (High-Contrast Fill):**
  - **On Dark Canvas:** Solid white background (`#FFFFFF`), solid black typography (`#0D0D0D`), uppercase `label-caps`, 0px border radius, zero shadow.
  - **On Light Canvas:** Solid obsidian background (`#0D0D0D`), alabaster text (`#F9F9F8`).
  - **Hover State:** Background shifts to Gold (`#D4AF37`) with text transitioning to `#0D0D0D`; smooth 200ms cubic-bezier transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Transparent background bounded by a 1px hairline border matching the active context foreground. On hover, background fills with 5% ambient tint and border illuminates in Gold.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- Full-bleed or framed rectilinear frames.
- Image containers feature a slow zoom interaction (`transform: scale(1.03)` over 600ms ease-out) on hover.
- Captions and event metadata use `label-caps` positioned directly beneath or floating flush in the lower left corner against a subtle dark vignette.

### Input Fields & Contact Consultation Form
- Understated architectural underline fields: no bounding box, only a 1px bottom border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on dark mode, `rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.2)` on light mode).
- Focus state transforms the underline into solid Gold (`#D4AF37`) with an animated width draw.
- Floating placeholder transitions smoothly into a small uppercase label (`label-caps`).

### Chips & Badges
- Ultra-slim rectilinear pill-less indicators with 1px border.
- Used for event categories (`BODA CIVIL`, `GALA DE GRADUACIÓN`, `RECEPCIÓN DE GALA`) and date timestamps. No solid backgrounds; pure typographic presence.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Sharp 0px square containers. Active states display a solid interior Gold fill or a crisp diagonal stroke instead of standard round checkmarks.

### Specialized Event Components
- **Curated Lookbook Grid:** Asymmetrical dual-column photo stream where portrait shots occupy 7 columns and landscape vignettes occupy 5 columns, separated by generous whitespace.
- **Chronological Milestones (Itinerary / Timeline):** Single hairline vertical gold axis with minimal serif or condensed sans-serif hour markers and discreet descriptive copy.
- **Private Inquiry Concierge Bar:** Persistent bottom dock or top header featuring an unadorned direct WhatsApp / Private Concierge callout in tracked all-caps.
